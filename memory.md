# IIM Bangalore Hackathon — Memory

## Overview
A 30-hour-ish hackathon trip to IIM Bangalore for Rohit, Ankush, and Udit — one overnight
train ride, one campus prank, a lot of building, one legendary nap, and no trophy at the end
of it. Exactly the kind of trip that becomes a better story than the hackathon itself.

## Timeline Narrative
It started with two very different arrivals. Rohit and Ankush had been up all night in a
general coach [[ast_0001]], barely functioning by the time they reached campus — while Udit,
who already lives in Bangalore, showed up looking suspiciously well-rested [[ast_0002]]. First
order of business, obviously, wasn't the hackathon — it was standing under the IIM B sign
[[ast_0003]] and sending the photo to the class group chat to convince everyone they'd gotten
in.

Since they'd arrived early, there was time to just walk the campus [[ast_0004]] before
registration, including a stop at the water tank made famous by *3 Idiots* [[ast_0005]] —
practically a required photo op for anyone visiting.

Then the actual hackathon started [[ast_0006]], heads down, laptops open. Sometime past
midnight there was a quick detour back to the water tank area for photos on the equally
famous "butt chairs" [[ast_0007]], before the team had to head off to a hotel — staying
overnight on campus wasn't allowed. Next morning, they were back on campus picking up right
where they left off [[ast_0008]].

By the afternoon of day two, the sleep deprivation caught up — Rohit was found completely
dozed off in his chair, laptop still open [[ast_0009]], a fitting end-of-hackathon photo.
